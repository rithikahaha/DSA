# 238. Product of Array Except Self
LeetCode: https://leetcode.com/problems/product-of-array-except-self/ · Medium · Pattern: Prefix / postfix products

## Problem
Given a list `nums`, return a new list where each position holds **the product of all the other numbers** (everything except the number at that position). You may **not use division**.

```
Input:  nums = [1, 2, 4, 6]
Output: [48, 24, 12, 8]
   position 0: 2 × 4 × 6 = 48     position 1: 1 × 4 × 6 = 24
   position 2: 1 × 2 × 6 = 12     position 3: 1 × 2 × 4 = 8
```

## Words you need first
| Word | Plain-English meaning |
|---|---|
| **product** | The result of multiplying numbers together. |
| **prefix** | Everything **to the left** of a position. |
| **postfix** | Everything **to the right** of a position. |

## How to come up with it (the thinking)
**Step 1: the obvious idea.** For each position, multiply all the other numbers with a loop. With n numbers that's n × n multiplications.

**Step 2: the "cheat" idea.** Multiply everything, then divide by the number at each position. But division is banned, and it also breaks if the list contains a 0.

**Step 3: the trick.** For any position, "everything except me" = **(everything to my left) × (everything to my right)**. So do two sweeps:
- Sweep 1, left to right: for each position, record the product of everything to its left.
- Sweep 2, right to left: multiply in the product of everything to its right.

**Step 4: a detail.** In each sweep we **save first, then update the running product**. That keeps the current number out of its own answer.

## Solution
```python
class Solution:
    def productExceptSelf(self, nums: List[int]) -> List[int]:
        res = [1] * (len(nums))

        prefix = 1
        for i in range(len(nums)):
            res[i] = prefix
            prefix *= nums[i]
        postfix = 1
        for i in range(len(nums) - 1, -1, -1):
            res[i] *= postfix
            postfix *= nums[i]
        return res
```

## Line by line
| Line | What it does |
|---|---|
| `res = [1] * len(nums)` | The answer list, filled with 1s to start (multiplying by 1 changes nothing). |
| `prefix = 1` | Running product of everything to the left. Nothing to the left yet, so 1. |
| `for i in range(len(nums)):` | Sweep 1: positions from left to right. |
| `res[i] = prefix` | Save the left-product for this position. |
| `prefix *= nums[i]` | **Then** include this number, ready for the next position. |
| `postfix = 1` | Running product of everything to the right. Nothing yet, so 1. |
| `for i in range(len(nums) - 1, -1, -1):` | Sweep 2: positions from the **last** down to 0. |
| `res[i] *= postfix` | Multiply the saved left-product by the right-product. |
| `postfix *= nums[i]` | Include this number for the position to its left. |

## Watch it run
`nums = [1, 2, 4, 6]`

**Sweep 1 (left → right):**

| `i` | `nums[i]` | `res[i] = prefix` | `prefix` after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 4 | 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 6 | 8 | 48 |

`res` is now `[1, 1, 2, 8]` (the product of everything on each number's left).

**Sweep 2 (right → left):**

| `i` | `nums[i]` | `res[i]` before | `postfix` used | `res[i]` after | `postfix` after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 6 | 8 | 1 | 8 | 6 |
| 2 | 4 | 2 | 6 | 12 | 24 |
| 1 | 2 | 1 | 24 | 24 | 48 |
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 48 | 48 | 48 |

Final `res = [48, 24, 12, 8]` ✅

## How fast is it?
- **Time: O(n).** Two passes over the list.
- **Space: O(1) extra.** The answer list doesn't count; we only use two extra variables.
